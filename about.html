WELCOME TO inSpiRe

Building Skills. Inspiring Careers.

Welcome to inSpiRe Medical Coding Institute, a medical coding training institute located in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh.

We focus on helping students develop the knowledge, practical skills, and confidence needed to explore career opportunities in the healthcare industry.

Our training programs include CPC, CCS, Medical Billing, Pharmacovigilance, and specialized medical coding courses.

Through structured instruction, practical exercises, and career guidance, we aim to support students throughout their learning journey.
